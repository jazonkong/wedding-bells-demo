## Overview

Wedding Bells is an all-in-one wedding planning platform prototype designed to help couples manage their wedding journey from engagement through to wedding day.

The platform combines:

- Vendor discovery
- Wedding planning
- Budget tracking
- Timeline management
- Guest management
- Messaging
- Document storage
- Payment tracking

into a single experience.

---

## Vision

Wedding Bells bridges the gap between couples and wedding vendors by providing one central place to:

- Discover and compare vendors
- Manage budgets
- Track deposits and payments
- Store contracts, invoices and receipts
- Organise guests and invitations
- Follow a personalised wedding roadmap
- Receive intelligent reminders
- Manage all wedding communications in one place
